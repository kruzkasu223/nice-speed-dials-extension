# Nice Speed Dials

### A fast, calm, and customizable new-tab page powered by your browser bookmarks.

[Visit Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/nice-speed-dials/igdancpfkcmgelecddchfeijbofdcnaa)
[Visit Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/nice-speed-dials/)
[Visit Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/nice-speed-dials/bficopaolgfhkccfcnmfphkmnacekbil)

Nice Speed Dials is a browser extension With a sleek and intuitive interface
that allows you to organize your browser's new tab page with your favourite
websites and links. With just a few clicks, you can create and organize a
collection of speed dials that can be accessed from any new tab you open.

Unlike other speed dial extensions or the default new tab page, Nice Speed Dials
stores your speed dials as bookmarks in your browser account. This means that
even if you change devices or uninstall the extension, your speed dials will
remain safe and accessible. Simply log in to your browser account on any device,
and your speed dials will be there waiting for you.

IMPORTANT: Please remember that your speed dials are not stored on our server,
but in your own browser's bookmarks. So please do not delete the bookmark folder
"NICE_SPEED_DIALS_BOOKMARKS\_[DO_NOT_DELETE]", as this is where your speed dials
are stored. If you delete this folder, you will lose your speed dials. or any
update in that folder will be reflected in your new tab speed dials. However,
you can use this to your advantage by saving any bookmark to this folder, and it
will appear on your new tab speed dials!

Try Nice Speed Dials today and streamline your browsing experience!

---

### Features:

- Sleek and intuitive interface
- Create and organize speed dials
- Create folders and organize speed dials in nested folder hierarchies
- Choose searchable icons for folders
- Add to speed dials from any tab
- Speed dials are stored in your browser bookmarks
- Sync speed dials across multiple devices (if your browser sync is on)
- Reorder speed dials via drag and drop
- Keyboard accessibility
- Many options for customization
- Nice animation on any actions
